About the Author

A life
in words.

Pooja Malhotra is an author and storyteller whose work explores human relationships, identity, memory and the beautifully complicated spaces between people.

Her writing is rooted in observation and emotion — finding extraordinary stories in ordinary lives and giving quiet voices a place to be heard.

Through fiction, essays and personal reflections, Pooja invites readers to slow down, look closer and discover something of themselves in every story.
