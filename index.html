Common Questions

How fast can we see results?

The workflow audit takes one week. The first engine typically runs in shadow mode by week 3–4, and goes live once accuracy is proven against your real data. Most clients see measurable time savings within the first month.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. Our overlays and bots wrap around what you already run — modern SaaS, legacy consoles, or custom in-house tools. If a human can operate it, our machines can too.

How is our data handled?

Processing can run fully localized inside your perimeter. Every bot action is logged, every decision traceable, and AI agents operate inside guardrails compiled from your own SOPs.

What if a bot hits something it doesn't understand?

Exceptions route to a human-in-the-loop dashboard with full context attached. Nothing silently fails, and the exception rate itself is a metric we contractually track.

What does engagement pricing look like?

The audit produces a per-workflow ROI ranking first, so you decide with numbers on the table. Builds are scoped per engine — ask us for the model for your use case.
